The business scenario

The board asks: what is the business worth, what happens if growth stalls, and how much cash can we return? A 1,080-row units × price revenue build feeds a fully linked model; one dropdown switches the whole model between Base, Upside and Downside, and the DCF, sensitivity grid and summary update instantly. FY24–FY26 actuals tie to the FP&A pack.

05

How it is built

  • CFI colour convention: blue = hardcoded input, black = same-sheet formula, green = cross-sheet link — trace any number at a glance.
  • Scenario switch: one dropdown (01_Exec_Summary!C4) re-points every driver via INDEX; no macros.
  • No circular references: interest is calculated on opening balances; the balance sheet balances to $0.00 in every year.
  • Sensitivity grid uses a closed-form formula in every cell (not a data table), so it recalculates instantly in Excel and LibreOffice.
  • FY26 revenue and EBITDA tie to the FP&A workbook within rounding (checked on 09_Checks).
