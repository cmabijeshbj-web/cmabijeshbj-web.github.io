What it does

A monthly revenue build for 5 product lines across 6 regions over 36 months feeds an annual 3-statement model (Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow) for FY24–FY26, fully formula-linked — change an assumption and the whole model recalculates. The balance sheet ties to exactly $0 every year. A DCF sheet derives unlevered free cash flow, discounts it at an 11% WACC, and computes enterprise and implied equity value with a Gordon Growth terminal value.
