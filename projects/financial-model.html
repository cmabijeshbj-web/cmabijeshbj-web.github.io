What it does

A monthly revenue build for 5 product lines across 6 regions over 36 months — Units × Avg Price, fully formula-driven — feeds an annual 3-statement model (Income Statement, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow) for FY24–FY26, fully formula-linked — change an assumption and the whole model recalculates. The balance sheet ties to exactly $0 every year. A DCF sheet derives unlevered free cash flow, discounts it at an 11% WACC, computes enterprise and implied equity value with a Gordon Growth terminal value, and includes a 5×5 sensitivity table showing enterprise value across a range of WACC and terminal-growth assumptions. Every sheet displays figures in abbreviated $K / $M form and has gridlines turned off for a cleaner, presentation-ready look.

The workbook follows CFI (Corporate Finance Institute) formatting conventions throughout: a Cover / Model Map sheet with a color legend and clickable navigation to every tab; blue for every hardcoded input, black for formulas referencing the same sheet, and green for any formula that links to a different sheet — so you can trace exactly where every number comes from at a glance. A dedicated 07_Checks sheet consolidates the model's integrity checks (balance sheet balances, cash flow ties to the balance sheet, net income flows correctly through all three statements) into a single pass/fail status.
