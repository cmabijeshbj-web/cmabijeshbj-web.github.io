The business scenario

A UAE mainland company prepares its FY2025 corporate-tax return. The CFO wants to know why the effective rate is below 9%, what drives the add-backs, whether the interest-limitation rule bites, and what tax would look like if profit moves. Every ledger line carries a tax-treatment code, so the computation is traceable from GL to return.

How it is built

  • Treatment codes (TAXABLE, EXEMPT, ALLOW, DIS100, DIS50, INTLIM, TP) on each ledger line drive every adjustment through a lookup — fully traceable.
  • Interest-limitation test, donations cap and 75% loss-utilisation cap are formulas with parameters on a single Parameters sheet.
  • Rate reconciliation proves the computation: notional 9% tax ± each driver = net CT payable (checked to AED 0).
  • Sensitivity is closed-form and ties to the base computation.
  • Rules summarised from Federal Decree-Law No. 47 of 2022 for illustration — confirm against current law and Ministerial Decisions before filing.
