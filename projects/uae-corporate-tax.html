What it does

1,090 transaction-level adjusting entries for FY2025 — disallowed expenses (entertainment, fines), add-backs (accounting depreciation, unrealized provisions), deductions (tax depreciation, realized bad debts), and exempt income (dividends, government grants). The computation sheet builds from accounting net profit to taxable income, then applies the UAE CT Law's 0% band up to AED 375,000 and 9% above it.
