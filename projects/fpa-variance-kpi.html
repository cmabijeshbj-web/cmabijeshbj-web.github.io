The business scenario

Meridian Technology Services (an illustrative IT-services group) closed FY26 with revenue growing 7.4% — but missing budget. The finance team needs to know in one page: where did profit fall short, is it volume or cost, which units and regions are responsible, and is the budgeting process itself biased? This workbook answers those questions and refreshes by changing a single cell (fiscal year).

05

How it is built

  • Every KPI is a SUMIFS / formula over the Data sheet — no pasted values; change the fiscal year in Exec_Summary!C4 and all sheets, charts and commentary update.
  • RAG thresholds (−2% / −5%, margin −0.5 / −1.5 pp) are editable inputs on the summary sheet.
  • Variance bridge is an exact decomposition: budget OI + volume + direct-cost rate + opex rate = actual OI (verified in Checks).
  • Revenue ties to the Integrated Financial Model and the SQL billing register — one version of the truth across projects.
  • Gridlines off, Calibri throughout, short $K / $M number formats with correct signs, conditional formatting for signed variances and RAG.
