What it does

A monthly Budget-vs-Actual operating P&L across 5 business units, 6 regions, and 36 months (Apr 2023–Mar 2026). Every variance — revenue, direct cost, opex, operating income — is a live formula referencing the source data, with an automatic flag on any month where operating income missed budget by more than 10%. A KPI dashboard sheet summarizes revenue, gross margin %, operating margin %, and adverse-month counts by business unit, plus a company-wide monthly trend chart.
