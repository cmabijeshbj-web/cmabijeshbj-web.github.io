Interactive Executive FP&A Dashboard

A Power BI-style executive dashboard with three filters — fiscal year, business unit and region — that drive six KPI tiles, six charts, a BU × region heatmap, a receivables table and auto-written commentary. Built as a live web page on the same data as the FP&A workbook, so every number ties.

What you can do with it

FeatureWhat happens when you change a filter
KPI tilesRevenue, gross margin, operating income, operating margin, revenue per head and budget accuracy — each with variance to budget, RAG dot and YoY growth
Auto-written commentaryPlain-English key messages: revenue vs budget, OI shortfall split into volume / cost-rate / opex-rate, weakest and strongest BU and region
Trend chartsMonthly revenue and operating income, budget vs actual, for the selected scope
Variance bridgeWaterfall from budget OI to actual OI using the same exact decomposition as the Excel workbook
Segment chartsRevenue by business unit and operating margin by region
HeatmapOperating margin for every BU × region combination, colour-scaled
ReceivablesOpen-invoice ageing at 31-Mar-2026 and the largest overdue balances, filtered by BU and region

Built to tie

FY26 revenue $171.7M (-2.3% vs budget) and operating income $39.6M (-8.4%) on the dashboard are identical to the Excel scorecard and the PDF board pack. The dashboard is regenerated by the same build script, so it can never drift from the workbook. The data is also provided as CSV for Power BI or Excel.
