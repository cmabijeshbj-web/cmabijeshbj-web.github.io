What it does

An interactive analytics dashboard styled after a Power BI report — KPI cards, a business-unit filter, and monthly trend charts — built as a live web page (HTML/JS) on the same 1,080-row FP&A dataset used in the Variance & KPI project, since Power BI Desktop isn't available in this build environment. Selecting a business unit from the filter live-updates every KPI card and all four charts (Revenue Budget vs Actual, Operating Income trend, Revenue by BU, and Operating Margin % by BU) to that unit's own numbers. The underlying dataset is provided as a CSV so it can also be opened directly in Power BI or Excel.
