About Me

I am Bijesh BJ, a CMA (USA)-certified finance professional based in Dubai, UAE.

My career began in accounting and tax compliance, where I built the foundations that still shape my work: accuracy, documentation and respect for the audit trail. I then moved into FP&A, working on budgeting, forecasting, variance analysis and management reporting for a global technology and IT services business.

That route, from the ledger to the boardroom, is my advantage. I understand where the numbers come from, so I can explain what they mean. It is backed by a B.Com from the University of Kerala, an M.Com and a PG Diploma in International Business Operations from IGNOU, the CMA (USA) designation, and the IMA Data Analytics & Visualization certificate.

I believe finance should be accurate, automated and decision-oriented. That is why I invest in Power BI, SQL, SAP S/4HANA FICO and AI-assisted workflows, and why every project on this site is built to answer a leadership question rather than only to fill a report.

I am open to FP&A and business finance opportunities in the UAE, where I can bring this combination of accounting discipline, analytics and automation to a growing team.
