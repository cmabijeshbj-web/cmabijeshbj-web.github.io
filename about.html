About Me

I am a CMA (USA)-certified Finance and FP&A professional with 4+ years of experience across business finance, management accounting, financial analysis, P&L management and tax compliance. I currently work in FP&A at UST, supporting performance reporting, forecasting and variance analysis.

My focus is the intersection of finance, data and technology. I analyse revenue, margins, costs and profitability to find what drives performance, and I present the findings as clear, executive-ready reporting that supports sound decisions.

I pair this with practical skills in advanced Excel, Power BI, SQL and SAP S/4HANA FICO. I am also grounded in IFRS, US GAAP, Indian GST, UAE VAT and UAE Corporate Tax, so I understand how reporting, compliance and the underlying transactions connect.

I also apply AI to finance automation, streamlining repetitive reporting and data workflows so finance teams spend less time preparing numbers and more time interpreting them.
